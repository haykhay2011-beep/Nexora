Night shift · Saltmarsh County

Port Veyra

A harbor town after dark. Take parcel jobs at the amber markers and beat the clock to the drop-off. You start with a pistol; heavier guns are lying around town. Shooting, hitting people or ramming cars raises your heat. At one star the patrol cars come for you, and from two stars they shoot back.
