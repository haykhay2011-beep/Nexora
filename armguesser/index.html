    How to play

    1. Look around. The left panel shows a satellite view of a mystery place in Armenia. Drag and zoom to hunt for clues: rivers, lakes, road shapes, farmland, mountains.
    2. Drop a pin. Click the map of Armenia on the right where you think it is. Keyboard: focus the map, pan with arrow keys, press G to pin the centre.
    3. Guess. Press the button (or Space). You score 5000 × (1 − km / 400) — a perfect guess is 5,000.
    4. Five rounds. Press N for the next round. Max score is 25,000.

    Tip: Lake Sevan, the Ararat plain and the deep Syunik gorges are easy to spot from above.
