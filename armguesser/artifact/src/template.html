    About the game

    armguesser is a geography game about Armenia.

    Each round names a place in Armenia: a monastery, a town, a lake, a village up in the hills. You pin it on a map of the country. The closer you land, the more you score. Five rounds, 25,000 points to play for.

    How to play

    1. Read the place. You get its English name, its Armenian spelling and transliteration, and what kind of place it is.
    2. Drop a pin. Click the map where you think it is, and move the pin as often as you like. Stuck? Show the region highlights the right region, but that round scores 25% less.
    3. Guess. Your pin turns blue, the answer appears in green, and a dashed line shows the distance between them, along with a fact about the place.
    4. Repeat. After five rounds you see your total, a rank, a map of all five rounds and a score card you can copy.

    Keyboard: focus the map and pan with the arrow keys. G pins the centre, H shows the region, Space guesses, N goes to the next round.

    Scoring

    Each round is worth up to 5,000 points. Your score falls in a straight line with distance and reaches zero at 400 km, roughly the length of Armenia from the Georgian border to the Iranian one.

    points = 5000 × (1 − distance in km ÷ 400)

    Difficulty

    Every place has a rating from one to four stars. Your mode decides which ratings come up and how much the map gives away. Within a mode, places from your recent games are held back so you see fresh ones first.

    ModePlaces drawn fromPoolMap

    Daily challenge

    The daily challenge uses the date to pick five places from the whole database, so everyone who plays on the same day gets the same five. Your first daily game each day counts toward your streak. Play on consecutive days to grow it; miss a day and it restarts at one. Replays that day are for practice and don’t change your score.

        Your data

        There are no accounts and nothing is sent anywhere. Your leaderboard, achievements, streak and settings stay in your own browser, and clearing its site data resets them. Other players never see them.

        How to play

        1. Each round names a place in Armenia, with its Armenian spelling and what kind of place it is.
        2. Click the map where you think it is. Keyboard: focus the map, pan with the arrow keys, press G to pin the centre.
        3. Press Guess or Space. You score 5000 × (1 − km ÷ 400), so a perfect pin is 5,000.
        4. Stuck? Show the region (or H) highlights it on the map, but that round scores 25% less.
        5. Press N for the next round. Five rounds, 25,000 points max.

        Easy shows region names, Medium shows region borders, and Hard and Expert give you a blank map.
