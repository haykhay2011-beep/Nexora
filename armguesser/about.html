About the game

armguesser is a geography game about Armenia.

You get a satellite view of a mystery place somewhere in Armenia. You drop a pin where you think it is. The closer you land, the more you score. Five rounds take you from the streets of Yerevan to the gorges of Syunik.

How to play

  1. Look around. The left panel is a satellite view of the mystery place. Drag and zoom to look for clues: rivers, lakes, the shape of roads, farmland, forest, mountains.
  2. Drop a pin. Click the map of Armenia on the right where you think the place is. You can move the pin as often as you like.
  3. Guess. Your pin turns blue, the answer appears in green, and a dashed line shows the distance between them. You also get the place’s Armenian name and a fact about it.
  4. Repeat. Five rounds make a game. At the end you get your total, a rank, your stats and a score card to share.

Scoring

Each round is worth up to 5,000 points. Your score drops in a straight line with distance and reaches zero at 400 km, roughly the length of Armenia from the Georgian border to the Iranian one.

points = 5000 × (1 − distance in km ÷ 400)

Difficulty

Every place in the game has a difficulty rating from one to four stars. The mode you pick decides which ratings you’ll see, how zoomed-in the satellite view starts, and how far you can pan away from the target.

ModePlaces drawn fromPoolView

Easy also shows what kind of place you’re looking at (a monastery, a lake, a town). Within a mode, places you’ve seen in recent games are held back so you get fresh ones first.

Daily challenge

The daily challenge picks five places from the whole database using the date as the seed. Everyone who plays on the same day gets the same five, so you can compare scores with friends.

Your first daily game each day counts toward your streak. Play on consecutive days to grow it; miss a day and it restarts at one. You can replay the day’s challenge for practice, but only the first score counts.

      Your data

      There are no accounts and no tracking. Your leaderboard, achievements, streak, settings and recently seen places are saved in your own browser’s local storage and never leave your device. Clearing your browser’s site data resets them.

      The game loads map tiles from Esri and CARTO and the map library from cdnjs, so those services see the usual request information, such as your IP address.
